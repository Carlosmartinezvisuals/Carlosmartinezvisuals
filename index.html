Stories to tell
 

London-based freelance videographer and content creator with over a decade of commercial experience. I've produced work for some of the world's most recognisable brands — from Nissan to Rémy Martin, BBC to David Beckham's Haig Club. Every project starts with a story.

My approach is cinematic by instinct and strategic by design. I see every brief as a narrative waiting to be shaped — with texture, rhythm, and a clear point of view. Bilingual in English and Spanish, I bring versatility across markets and cultures.

I shoot on Sony systems and work across DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, After Effects, Photoshop, and Lightroom. Currently available for new commercial projects, retainers, and in-house or agency roles.
